# Third-party provenance

The maintained compressible-Euler driver in `examples/euler/euler.cpp` and its
shared DG operator in `examples/euler/euler.hpp` are derived from MFEM Example
18 and have been substantially extended for the methods and experiments in this
repository. MFEM also supplies all finite-element, mesh, parallel, and
visualization infrastructure used by the examples.
