\section{Experimental Methodology}
\label{sec:experimental-methodology}

The numerical study is currently in its design phase.  The final manifest is
therefore marked \emph{draft} and cannot be executed by the study runner.  A
dry run expands every proposed row, checks its metadata, and estimates its cost
from development measurements without launching a numerical solver.  Final
measurements will begin only after the matrix has been reviewed and its status
is changed explicitly to \emph{approved}.

\subsection{Comparison contract}

The headline methods are conventional RKDG, adapted OFDG restricted by KXRCF,
and the 2024 OEDG comparator.  All-cell adapted OFDG is a targeted ablation.
For a given row, the methods share the governing problem, mesh, polynomial
degree, numerical flux, CFL rule, final time, and, where required, the common
positivity treatment.  Comparisons across degrees are reported both at equal
mesh and at approximately equal actual global degrees of freedom; these answer
different questions and are not combined.

Smooth problems record $L^1$, $L^2$, and $L^\infty$ errors.  For uniform
refinement from $h$ to $h/2$, the observed rate is
\begin{equation}
  r=\frac{\log(E_h/E_{h/2})}{\log 2}.
  \label{eq:observed-rate}
\end{equation}
The 1D advection superconvergence experiment additionally records the
cell-average and downwind-point errors defined in
\cref{sec:results-convergence-tables}.  Discontinuous cases record independent
reference error, total variation, extrema, overshoot, undershoot, conservation
drift, troubled- and limited-cell activity, rejected steps, and failure time.

Timing is separate from accuracy.  Proposed timing rows specify one unrecorded
warm-up and five repetitions, to be summarized by the median and interquartile
range.  Fixed-work strong scaling uses one, two, and four MPI ranks.  A pilot
will select a workload whose one-rank time is long enough to dominate startup
cost.  No timing value from the reduced CI checkpoint is interpreted as a
performance result.

\subsection{Proposed experiment families}

The approval matrix includes smooth $P^1$--$P^3$ sequences for one- and
two-dimensional advection, pre-shock Burgers flow, the one- and two-dimensional
Euler waves, the transported vortex, and a triangular-mesh sequence.  The
discontinuous group contains post-shock Burgers flow, scaled Lax data,
Woodward--Colella blast waves~\cite{WoodwardColella1984}, the Shu--Osher
shock--entropy interaction~\cite{ShuOsher1989}, and a two-dimensional Euler
Riemann problem~\cite{LaxLiu1998}.

Targeted ablations vary the KXRCF threshold, compare all-cell and gated OFDG,
compare physical and unit wave-speed scaling, and compare stage-wise with
post-step OFDG cadence.  Robustness is measured first without assistance and
then with the common positivity limiter.  The exact proposed meshes, final
times, metrics, references, estimated costs, and intended report artifacts are
listed in \cref{app:study-design}; they remain proposals rather than completed
experiments.

\subsection{Reproducibility and provenance}

Each eventual row will record its full command, actual DOFs, time-integration
parameters, MPI ranks, source revision, MFEM revision, compiler, host, and UTC
timestamp.  Raw logs and rank-local profiles are retained.  Derived CSV,
tables, and figures are generated in a separate deterministic analysis step.
The software implementation uses MFEM for meshes, finite element spaces,
parallel ownership, and operator assembly~\cite{AndersonEtAl2021}.
