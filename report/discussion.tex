\chapter{Discussion}
\label{chap:discussion}
\section{What the present evidence supports}
The implementation and the preview answer different questions. The operator
checks establish that the represented filtering operation preserves constants
and component means to roundoff, leaves inactive cells unchanged, and behaves
consistently across tested coefficient bases and MPI partitions. The curved
construction extends those checks to static polynomial geometry using physical
quadrature and projected derivatives. It does not turn successively projected
derivatives into exact higher derivatives.

The evolution experiments show that this construction can be used in a
complete solver on modest meshes. For smooth P2 advection, gating reduces the
additional error of all-cell OFDG and approaches the DG error, including on the
curved sequence. On scalar shocks, both OFDG configurations suppress large
sampled excursions. The Shu--Osher wave detail suggests a useful tradeoff:
selective filtering retains more wave variation while requiring few positivity
interventions in this configuration. These findings motivate further targeted
experiments, rather than a universal claim that one method is best.

\section{What still prevents stronger interpretation}
A global activation count cannot show whether KXRCF chooses the appropriate
cells. Similar solutions may arise because the detector is selective, because
all-cell damping is already negligible in smooth regions, or because the test
is insufficiently discriminating. Spatial evidence is needed to separate these
explanations.
\MissingWork{KX-01}{Record spatial indicator values, threshold, and active cells
alongside a shock and a smooth-wave profile; test a small range of thresholds;
identify missed shocks and unnecessary smooth-region activation.}

OEDG is a useful comparator, but the present comparison changes both the
sensor and the cadence. The common Euler positivity treatment can further
alter the profile. Its event counts are therefore reported as part of the
algorithm's behaviour rather than attributed to the oscillation filter alone.
\MissingWork{TIME-01}{Compare post-step and stage-wise OFDG on a targeted scalar
and Euler case at controlled timesteps before attributing differences with
OEDG solely to the sensor. Record positivity activity with each comparison.}

The WENO sensitivity in \cref{tab:reference-sensitivity} is small in its
spatial mean but appreciable in its maximum. Consequently the current reference
is adequate for qualitative profile context, not a certification of small
shock errors. The requirement in placeholder REF-01 must be resolved before
publishing quantitative accuracy rankings based on those curves.

\section{Scope and next experiments}
The most informative next step is a focused study of detector selectivity and
Shu--Osher wave preservation, accompanied by reference refinement. A controlled
curved-mesh sequence with varied deformation would then test how robust the
present smooth-transport observation is. Larger Euler grids become useful
when they answer those specific questions and the reference and diagnostic
choices are settled.
\MissingWork{GEO-01}{For a claim of robust curved-mesh behaviour beyond the
current fixtures, vary mesh deformation and geometry order; report geometric
quality, quadrature sensitivity, and a targeted curved Euler comparison.}

\ScopeLimit{Native rational NURBS faces remain outside the verified MFEM/OFDG
path. Static polynomial curvilinear meshes are usable and already tested, so
removing this limitation is not a prerequisite for the planned experiments.
Moving meshes, variable solution order, pyramids, large 3D shock studies,
superconvergence, and parallel scaling are outside this preview.}

No general convergence, entropy-stability, or positivity theorem for the
adapted sensor is proved here. The frozen filtering map's algebraic
properties do not by themselves prove those properties for the nonlinear,
time-discrete conservation-law solver. Similarly, laptop wall times and two-rank
agreement do not establish scaling efficiency. These are explicit limits on
the claims, not results replaced by speculative text.
