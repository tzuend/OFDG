\chapter{Discussion}
\label{chap:discussion}

\ifdraftresults
This chapter is intentionally limited to the interpretation framework.  The
final discussion will be written only after the approved experiment matrix has
been executed.  It will answer the research questions in the order posed in
\cref{chap:methods}: the effect of physical wave-speed scaling, the selectivity
and cost of KXRCF gating, distributed-memory behavior, and the comparative
accuracy and robustness of RKDG, adapted OFDG--KXRCF, and OEDG.

The discussion will distinguish observations from established theory.  In
particular, conservation follows from preservation of cell averages, whereas
convergence, nonoscillatory behavior, and robustness of the modified sensor
must be supported by the final measurements.  It will also address temporal
error, reference-grid independence, positivity-limiter intervention, detector
threshold sensitivity, implementation-specific bases and fluxes, and the
limited rank count of the local scaling study.  Basis independence will be
distinguished from geometric generality: the projection construction does not
prescribe a coefficient basis, whereas the current verified implementation
still assumes fixed, affine, uniform-order scalar polynomial DG elements.
