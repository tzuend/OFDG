\begin{abstract}
  This thesis studies maintainable oscillation-control mechanisms for
  discontinuous Galerkin discretizations of hyperbolic conservation laws in
  MFEM.  It develops an adapted oscillation-free discontinuous Galerkin method
  with face-local characteristic speeds, restricts that method using the KXRCF
  troubled-cell detector, and implements the scale- and evolution-invariant
  2024 OEDG method as a fixed comparator.  Polynomial-shell damping is realized
  through finite-element projection operators, so the stored solution basis
  need not be modal, orthogonal, or Legendre-based.  The implementation supports
  scalar equations and compressible Euler flow on distributed affine meshes and
  is accompanied by conservative admissibility treatment, an independent WENO5
  reference solver, and deterministic serial and MPI tests.

  The comparative numerical study is intentionally not finalized in this
  draft.  Its proposed design separates smooth accuracy, discontinuity
  resolution, detector selectivity, invariance, robustness, computational cost,
  and parallel scaling.  Final quantitative claims will be inserted only after
  the experiment matrix has been reviewed and an approved, fully traceable
  dataset has been generated.  Reduced development calculations are retained
  solely to verify the reporting workflow and are not treated as thesis
  evidence.
\end{abstract}
