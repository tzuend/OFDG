\chapter{Methods}
\label{chap:methods}

This chapter defines the numerical methods before discussing their software
realization or empirical behavior.  The presentation begins with the common
RKDG baseline, then introduces adapted OFDG, KXRCF, and the independent 2024
OEDG comparator in that order.  Positivity treatment and reference solutions
are common experimental controls rather than parts of any one stabilization
method.

\section{Governing equations and conventional RKDG}
\label{sec:rkdg}

Consider a system of hyperbolic conservation laws
\begin{equation}
  \partial_t\boldsymbol u+\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol f(\boldsymbol u)=0
  \quad\text{in }\Omega\times(0,T],
  \qquad
  \boldsymbol u(\boldsymbol x,0)=\boldsymbol u_0(\boldsymbol x).
  \label{eq:conservation-law}
\end{equation}
Scalar advection and Burgers' equation use one component.  For compressible
Euler in $d$ dimensions,
\begin{equation}
 \boldsymbol u=(\rho,\rho\boldsymbol v,E)^\mathsf T,
 \qquad
 p=(\gamma-1)\left(E-\tfrac12\rho|\boldsymbol v|^2\right),
 \label{eq:euler-state}
\end{equation}
and the flux in direction $j$ is
\begin{equation}
 \boldsymbol f_j(\boldsymbol u)
 =\bigl(\rho v_j,
        \rho v_j\boldsymbol v+p\boldsymbol e_j,
        (E+p)v_j\bigr)^\mathsf T.
 \label{eq:euler-flux}
\end{equation}

Let $V_h^k$ be the discontinuous polynomial space on
$\mathcal T_h$.  The conventional semidiscrete DG method seeks
$\boldsymbol u_h(t)\in[V_h^k]^m$ such that, on every element $K$,
\begin{equation}
 \int_K\partial_t\boldsymbol u_h\cdot\boldsymbol w_h\,\mathrm dx
 -\int_K\boldsymbol f(\boldsymbol u_h):\nabla\boldsymbol w_h\,\mathrm dx
 +\int_{\partial K}\widehat{\boldsymbol f}
       (\boldsymbol u_h^-,\boldsymbol u_h^+,\boldsymbol n_K)
       \cdot\boldsymbol w_h^-\,\mathrm ds=0
 \label{eq:rkdg-weak-form}
\end{equation}
for all $\boldsymbol w_h\in[V_h^k]^m$.  The numerical experiments use the
same Rusanov-type interface flux for all compared methods.  Explicit
Runge--Kutta integration produces the RKDG baseline; OFDG and OEDG alter only
the post-stage or post-step state, not \cref{eq:rkdg-weak-form}.  This separation
follows the standard RKDG framework for nonlinear hyperbolic systems
\cite{CockburnShu1998,GottliebShu1998}.
